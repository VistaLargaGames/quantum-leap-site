Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email vistalargagames@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We aim to reply within a few days.

Controls on iPhone and iPad

  • Left stick: move. Drag on the right side of the screen: look around.
  • Jump, Fire and Tongue buttons: on both sides of the screen. Hold Tongue to swing, release to fling.
  • Auto fire: your laser fires by itself when an enemy is in your crosshair. You can turn it off in Options.
  • Power button: appears at the bottom centre when you hold a power-up. Hold it to keep using it.
  • Look speed and Gyro Look: in Options. Gyro Look adds a Center button under Pause.

Using a game controller

Pair a Bluetooth controller (Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo Switch Pro) in iOS Settings → Bluetooth. The touch controls hide while you use the controller and come back when you touch the screen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my progress?
Delete the app and install it again. This removes your saved stages and settings.

The game feels slow or the sound crackles.
Close other apps, restart your device, and make sure iOS is up to date. If it continues, email us with your device model.

Can I play without a controller?
Yes. The game is designed for touch controls.
