Privacy Policy

Quantum Leap by Vista Larga Games · Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version

Quantum Leap does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (which stages you have unlocked, best scores) and your settings (volume, look speed and similar options) in a small file on your own device. This file is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this file.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services run inside the game.

Sensors and controllers

If you turn on Gyro Look in Options, the game reads your device's motion sensor while you play, to turn the camera. This data is used only on the device, in the moment, and is not stored or sent anywhere. If you connect a game controller, the game reads its buttons and sticks the same way, and can make it vibrate.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media services. Apple processes App Store purchases and downloads under its own privacy policy.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
